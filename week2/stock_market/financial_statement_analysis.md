## Basic Financial Calculations

### 1. Revenue Growth

**Formula:**  
Revenue Growth (%) = [(Current Year Revenue − Previous Year Revenue) ÷ Previous Year Revenue] × 100

= [(178,650 − 162,990) ÷ 162,990] × 100

= **9.61%**

**Interpretation:** Infosys's revenue from operations increased by approximately 9.61% in FY 2025–26.

### 2. Profit Margin

**Formula:**  
Profit Margin (%) = (Profit for the Year ÷ Revenue from Operations) × 100

| Financial year | Calculation | Profit margin |
|---|---|---:|
| FY 2024–25 | (26,750 ÷ 162,990) × 100 | 16.41% |
| FY 2025–26 | (29,474 ÷ 178,650) × 100 | 16.50% |

**Interpretation:** The profit-to-revenue ratio increased slightly from 16.41% to 16.50%. This indicates a small improvement in this measure between the two years.

## Overall Findings and Conclusion

### Key Findings

1. **Revenue growth:** Infosys's revenue from operations increased from ₹162,990 crore in FY 2024–25 to ₹178,650 crore in FY 2025–26, representing growth of approximately 9.61%.

2. **Profitability:** Profit for the year increased from ₹26,750 crore to ₹29,474 crore. The profit-to-revenue ratio also improved slightly from 16.41% to 16.50%.

3. **Financial position:** Total assets increased from ₹148,903 crore to ₹155,967 crore. However, total equity decreased from ₹96,203 crore to ₹93,297 crore, while current liabilities increased from ₹42,850 crore to ₹52,322 crore.

4. **Cash flow:** Cash generated from operating activities remained positive at ₹33,986 crore in FY 2025–26. Year-end cash and cash equivalents decreased from ₹24,455 crore to ₹22,201 crore.

### Conclusion

Based on the consolidated financial statements, Infosys reported higher revenue and profit in FY 2025–26 compared with FY 2024–25. Its profit-to-revenue ratio improved slightly, and it continued to generate positive cash from operating activities. At the same time, the increase in current liabilities and decrease in year-end cash are areas worth examining further. These figures should be considered alongside the company's business conditions, accounting notes and industry trends before drawing broader conclusions about its financial health.
